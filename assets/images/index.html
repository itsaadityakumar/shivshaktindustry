About Us

Shiv Shakti Industry

Shiv Shakti Industry

Est. 2022 | Bokaro, Jharkhand

Shiv Shakti Industry is a trusted name in the manufacturing of premium construction materials. Located in the industrial hub of Bokaro, Jharkhand, we specialize in producing high-quality fly ash bricks, paver blocks, parking tiles, cover blocks, and complete boundary solutions.

Our commitment to quality and sustainability has made us a preferred choice among builders, contractors, and homeowners. All our products are manufactured using state-of-the-art machinery and are 100% eco-friendly.
