About Shiv Shakti Industry

Built on Quality. Driven by Trust.

Shiv Shakti Industry is a leading manufacturing and construction-material supply company based in Telo, Bokaro (Jharkhand), focused on producing reliable construction products and providing dependable supply and transportation support.

Established in 2022, we operate heavy-duty automated hydraulic machinery to produce superior quality Fly Ash Bricks, Paver Blocks, Parking Tiles, and Precast Boundary Wall systems. We also provide authorized cement supply and operate our own dedicated transport fleet to deliver materials right to your job site on schedule.

Our mission is to be the most trusted partner for construction materials in Jharkhand, combining modern manufacturing technology with traditional business ethics of honesty, quality, and customer-first service.
