About Shiv Shakti Industry

Built on Quality. Driven by Trust.

Shiv Shakti Industry is a leading manufacturing and construction-material supply company based in Telo, Bokaro (Jharkhand), focused on producing reliable construction products and providing dependable supply and transportation support.

Established in 2022, we operate heavy-duty automated hydraulic machinery to produce superior quality Fly Ash Bricks, Paver Blocks, Parking Tiles, Concrete Cover Blocks, and Precast Boundary Wall systems. We also provide authorized cement supply and operate our own dedicated transport fleet to deliver materials right to your job site on schedule.

Quality First

Strict quality control with dense compaction, uniform sharp edges, and high load-bearing strength.

Reliable Supply

Continuous production line and extensive storage yard to meet regular and emergency site requirements.

Timely Delivery

In-house fleet of commercial tippers and delivery vehicles ensuring direct, on-time material arrival.

Customer Satisfaction

Transparent pricing, flexible scheduling, and dedicated support for builders, contractors, and individuals.
